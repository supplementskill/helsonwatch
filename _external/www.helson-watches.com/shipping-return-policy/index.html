Shipping

Watches will be shipped with FedEx from Hong Kong.

We are not responsible for delays caused through FedEx or customs and  we are also  not responsible for any customs or VAT/Tax you may possibly have to pay in your country

We try to ship within two business days.

All watches that can be ordered online are in stock and ready to ship, unless otherwise noted in the product description.

Please note that we can ship only to physical street addresses no PO boxes

You will receive a tracking number when shipped


Cancellation


As long as it has not shipped we can cancel your order with a full refund.

Please contact as immediately.

If you cancel your order after we shipped we have to deduct a 7% cancellation fee and  all the shipping costs

 
Returns

 If you like to return your watch  you can do that within three days after receipt.

Your shipping charges will not be refunded and  we have to deduct a 7% cancellation fee and $50 for the shipping costs we had.

Watches cannot be accepted for a refund if they have been worn  or if the strap/bracelet has been sized.
